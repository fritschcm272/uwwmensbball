What the numbers mean

  • Possession — FGA − OREB + TO + 0.475 × FTA. The 0.475 is KenPom's: it counts the share of free-throw attempts that end a trip, rather than Oliver's original 0.44.
  • Points a possession (PPP) — points ÷ possessions. On a play-call or lineup table it is the points scored on that set's or that unit's own tagged possessions.
  • Clutch — not the first half, 5:00 or less on the clock, and the margin 8 points or fewer — the same rule the clutch event log and the game plan use, applied to each team's own games.
  • Clutch, on offense — the possessions where he was the man with the ball. The points are the POSSESSION'S points, not his own scoring — a kick-out that someone else finishes counts here — and the rate appears once there are three of them. A trip that ends at the line scores nothing on the clip itself, so fouls drawn are counted separately.
  • Clutch, on defense — there is no individual defensive box score here, so the card separates the two: “HIM” is the one thing that is his — that he was on the floor for those possessions — and “THE FIVE HE WAS IN” is what the defense gave up while he was out there, a team number that belongs to all five. Lower is better, and the team's rate across every clutch defensive possession rides along for scale.
  • Clutch flags on a card — the gold ★ chips. “Best with the ball” is the highest points a trip among players with at least three clutch possessions of their own. “Best five on defense” is the fewest points allowed a possession with him on the floor, among players on for at least three and 40% of the team's clutch defensive possessions — a five-man number, so it credits the lineup he was in, not him alone. Neither is given when too few players clear the bar, or when everyone who does has the same number.
  • Leading / Trailing by 10+ — the margin is 10 or more, either way, outside the clutch window above.
  • eFG% — (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA — a three counted at its real worth.
  • TOV% — turnovers ÷ possessions.
  • ORB% — offensive rebounds ÷ (our offensive rebounds + their defensive rebounds) — the share of available misses rebounded.
  • FT Rate — FTA ÷ FGA, the Basketball-Reference form (how often they get to the line). Oliver's original uses FTM ÷ FGA, which measures getting there AND making them.
  • Four Factors ranking — the FOUR FACTORS table ranks the four at fixed weights — eFG% 40%, TOV% 25%, ORB% 20%, FT Rate 15% — Oliver's own order of importance. TEAM COMPARISON does something different: it prices each factor for THIS matchup, so the two can disagree about what matters most.
  • PPG difference — points scored minus points allowed, a game.
  • Min/G (lineups) — that unit's minutes on the floor divided by the games it appeared in — not its total minutes, and not minutes a game for the players in it.
  • Shot clock — seconds USED, scaled to a 30-second clock: Early 0–9, Organized 10–19, Late 20+.
  • “On film” / “tagged” — from the clips the coaches have tagged in the app, not the full season's box score — so these counts grow as tagging continues.
